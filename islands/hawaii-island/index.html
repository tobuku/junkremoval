Hawaii Island Junk Removal FAQ

Junk removal on Hawaii Island typically costs $175-$850 depending on load size and location. Hilo and Kona prices are similar — single items start at $85-$175, half truck loads run $375-$600, and full truck loads cost $550-$850. Remote areas like Ocean View or Pahoa may have surcharges due to distance from disposal facilities.
Most Big Island junk removal companies are based on either the Hilo (east) or Kona (west) side and primarily serve their local area. Some larger companies cover the entire island but may charge extra for cross-island trips. When requesting estimates, specify your exact location so providers can give accurate pricing.
Hawaii Island has several disposal facilities: the South Hilo Sanitary Landfill, the West Hawaii Sanitary Landfill near Kona, and transfer stations in Pahoa, Waimea, and other communities. The county also operates green waste and recycling drop-off sites at various locations across the island.
