Local Information

Oahu Disposal Resources

Key facilities, regulations, and resources for waste disposal on Oahu.

Transfer Stations

Oahu's primary disposal facility is the Waimanalo Gulch Sanitary Landfill. The City and County of Honolulu also operates several convenience centers and recycling drop-off sites across the island.

View Oahu transfer stations →

Recycling Centers

Oahu has multiple recycling drop-off locations accepting glass, metals, plastics, and electronics. HI-5 redemption centers are located island-wide for beverage container recycling.

View Oahu recycling centers →

Donation Centers

Goodwill Hawaii, Salvation Army, and Habitat for Humanity ReStore all accept furniture and household items on Oahu. Many offer free pickup for large donations.

View Oahu donation centers →

Disposal Regulations

Illegal dumping on Oahu carries fines up to $10,000. Appliances containing refrigerants require certified removal. E-waste must be disposed at designated facilities.

Oahu Junk Removal FAQ

Junk removal on Oahu typically costs $150-$800 depending on the load size. A single item pickup starts around $75-$150, a half truck load runs $350-$550, and a full truck load costs $500-$800. Estate cleanouts range from $1,500 to $5,000+. Oahu prices are generally 30-50% higher than mainland averages due to disposal fees and island logistics.
Most Oahu junk removal companies accept furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, yard waste, construction debris, and general household junk. Hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, and asbestos require specialized disposal. Some companies also handle hot tub removal, shed demolition, and full property cleanouts.
On Oahu, junk is taken to the Waimanalo Gulch Sanitary Landfill, PVT Land Company transfer station, or various recycling centers depending on the material. Responsible companies sort items for recycling, donation to organizations like Goodwill or Habitat ReStore, and only landfill what cannot be diverted.
