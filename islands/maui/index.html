Maui Junk Removal FAQ

Junk removal on Maui typically costs $175-$850 depending on the load size. Single item pickups start around $85-$175, half truck loads run $375-$600, and full truck loads cost $550-$850. Maui prices can be slightly higher than Oahu due to fewer providers and longer distances to disposal facilities.
Yes, several junk removal and debris cleanup companies serve the Lahaina area. Many have expanded services to handle fire damage cleanup, debris removal, and property clearing. Check with providers about their specific experience with fire damage cleanup and any special certifications they hold.
Maui's primary disposal facility is the Central Maui Landfill in Puunene. There are also transfer stations in Hana and other locations. Recyclable materials go to Maui Recycling Group and other certified recycling facilities. Donation-worthy items go to local charities like Habitat ReStore Maui.
Some Maui junk removal companies serve Hana, though availability is limited due to the remote location and long drive on the Hana Highway. Expect a surcharge for Hana service. Companies based in Central Maui may charge $100-$200 extra for Hana pickups. A few Hana-based operators also handle local jobs.
