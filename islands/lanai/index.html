Lanai Disposal Note

Lanai has a small landfill managed by Maui County with limited capacity. Appliances, electronics, vehicles, and hazardous materials typically need to be barged to Maui for proper disposal. Plan ahead for large cleanout projects — scheduling and logistics take longer on Lanai than any other island.
