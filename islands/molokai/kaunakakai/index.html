Kaunakakai Junk Removal & Hauling Services

Junk removal on Molokai is a different experience than anywhere else in Hawaii. Kaunakakai, the island's only town, has a population of roughly 3,500 and sits along a three-block main street that still feels like 1960s Hawaii. There are no big-box stores, no chain restaurants, and no fleet of junk hauling trucks. When residents need something removed, the options are limited to a handful of local operators — primarily Makoa Trucking — or doing it yourself with a pickup truck and a trip to the Molokai Landfill.

The biggest complication for junk removal on Molokai is disposal of large or restricted items. The Molokai Landfill accepts general household waste and some bulky items, but appliances containing refrigerants, electronics, tires in large quantities, and certain construction materials must be shipped off-island. That means loading items onto a Young Brothers barge at Kaunakakai Harbor for transport to Oahu — a process that adds $200–$500+ in shipping costs and requires advance scheduling.

Many Molokai residents rely on community cleanup days organized by the county to dispose of larger items at reduced or no cost. These events happen a few times per year and are the most affordable way to get rid of bulky junk. Between those events, DIY disposal is common: residents borrow or rent trucks, break down items themselves, and haul loads to the landfill. For those who cannot do it themselves — elderly residents, property managers handling vacation rentals, or families dealing with estate cleanouts — hiring a local hauler is essential.

If you are managing property on Molokai from off-island, coordinating junk removal requires extra lead time. Service windows are less flexible than on Oahu or Maui, and last-minute pickups are rarely possible. Planning ahead and bundling items into a single haul is the most cost-effective approach for Kaunakakai junk removal.

Kaunakakai Junk Removal Questions

Junk removal in Kaunakakai typically costs $200–$1,000+ depending on volume and whether barge transport is required. A single item pickup averages $100–$250, while a full truckload runs $600–$1,000. Prices are higher than on Oahu or Maui due to limited local haulers and barge shipping costs via Young Brothers.
Large items on Molokai that cannot be processed at the local landfill are shipped by barge to Oahu via Young Brothers for disposal or recycling. This adds cost and lead time to junk removal jobs. Some residents coordinate group shipments to reduce per-item barge fees.
Molokai has very limited junk removal options. Makoa Trucking is the primary local hauling company. Some residents handle disposal themselves using the Molokai Landfill. For larger jobs, companies from Maui may coordinate service with barge transport included.
Most junk from Kaunakakai is taken to the Molokai Landfill. Items that cannot be accepted locally — such as appliances requiring Freon removal, e-waste, and certain construction materials — must be barged to Oahu via Young Brothers for proper disposal or recycling.
