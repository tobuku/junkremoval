Junk Removal on Molokai

Molokai is Hawaii's most rural major island, with about 7,000 residents centered around Kaunakakai. Junk removal options are limited but available through local haulers and general contractors who offer cleanout services. For large projects, some Maui-based companies arrange barge transport for equipment and disposal.

Molokai Disposal Note

Molokai has a single landfill operated by Maui County. Certain materials like appliances, e-waste, and hazardous waste may need to be barged to Maui for proper disposal. This can add to the cost and timeline of junk removal projects on the island. Plan ahead for large cleanouts.

Molokai Junk Removal FAQ

Yes, there are a small number of junk removal and hauling providers on Molokai, mostly based in or near Kaunakakai. Due to the island's small population, options are limited compared to larger islands. Some Maui-based companies may also arrange service to Molokai via the barge.
Junk removal on Molokai costs $200-$1,000+ depending on the job. Prices are higher than larger islands due to limited providers, limited disposal facilities, and the cost of barging waste off-island for certain materials. Single item pickups start around $125-$250.
