Why It Has to Be Onsite

Phone Estimates and Photos Are Never Accurate

Junk removal pricing depends on factors that simply cannot be assessed remotely.

Access and Stairs Matter

Is the furniture on the third floor of a walk-up? Is there a narrow hallway, a locked gate, or a hillside driveway? These factors directly affect labor time and cost. A photo of a couch tells you nothing about how hard it is to get out of the building.

Photos Are Misleading

Camera angles, lighting, and framing make it easy to underestimate — or overestimate — the volume of a pile. What looks like a half-load in a photo can fill an entire truck once it's broken down and loaded. Inaccurate estimates lead to surprise charges on the day of pickup.

Volume Is Hard to Describe

Most people don't know how to estimate cubic yards or truck fractions. Saying "a garage full of stuff" or "maybe 10 bags" doesn't give a provider enough information to give you a firm, reliable price — especially for mixed loads with heavy items like appliances or concrete.

No Surprises on the Day

When a provider visits in person and gives you a firm price, that's what you pay. There are no upcharges when the crew arrives and sees stairs for the first time, no extra fees for a load that turned out heavier than described. Onsite estimates protect both you and the provider.
