Common Questions

Junk Removal Companies FAQ

Start by checking star ratings and review counts on this directory. Look for companies with 4.5+ stars and at least 20 reviews. Verify they are licensed and insured, ask about disposal methods, and get at least two quotes before booking. Local companies familiar with island logistics often provide better service than national chains.
Hawaii junk removal typically costs $150–$800 depending on load size. Single items start at $75–$150, a half truck runs $350–$550, and a full truck costs $500–$800. Island costs are 30–50% higher than the mainland due to disposal fees and limited landfill space. Outer island pricing may be slightly lower than Oahu.
Oahu, Maui, Kauai, and Hawaii Island all have multiple junk removal companies. Molokai has limited service with one primary provider. Lanai currently has no dedicated junk removal companies listed. For smaller islands, some Maui-based companies may offer periodic service trips.
Most companies accept furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, yard waste, construction debris, and general household junk. Hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, asbestos, and certain e-waste require specialized disposal. Always confirm accepted items when getting a quote.
Many companies on Oahu and Maui offer same-day or next-day pickup, especially those listing 24-hour availability. On Kauai and Hawaii Island, next-day service is more common. Same-day service may cost $25–$50 extra. Call early in the morning for the best availability.
