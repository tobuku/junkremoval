What a Listing Contains

Every listing is built from verified business data for accuracy and consistency.

Standard Listing Details

  • Business name and logo (if provided)
  • Street address and service area
  • Phone number with click-to-call link
  • Website link
  • Google rating and review count (pulled from Google Maps data)
  • Hours of operation (Monday through Sunday)
  • Islands and cities served
  • Service categories offered

Where Your Listing Appears

  • The main company directory at /companies/
  • Your island's browse page (e.g., /islands/oahu/)
  • Every city page within your service area
  • Relevant service category pages (junk removal, furniture removal, estate cleanouts, etc.)

The more service areas and categories you cover, the more pages your listing appears on.
